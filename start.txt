================================================================
GREEN LEAVES — Project Brief
24/7 Highway Restaurant & EV Charging · East–West Highway, Nepal
================================================================
(Working name: "Green Leaves". Renaming the whole site is a one-line
change — edit BUSINESS.name in src/data/plan.js.)

----------------------------------------------------------------
THE IDEA
----------------------------------------------------------------
A 24/7 restaurant + EV charging station on the East–West Highway
(Mahendra Rajmarg), between Butwal and Sunwal (the Butwal–Chitwan
stretch). Exact plot not chosen yet — three candidate sites are
being compared.

Target customer is NOT locals — it's road travellers (cars,
families, EV drivers, long-haul drivers) stopping for lunch,
dinner, snacks, coffee, and a charge. No capital yet; the plan is
to fund this with a business loan — so this site doubles as a
PITCH to lenders, partners, and family.

----------------------------------------------------------------
THE STRATEGIC SPINE (used throughout the site)
----------------------------------------------------------------
An EV charge takes ~30 minutes. During that window the driver and
family are a CAPTIVE audience. The business wins by maximizing
spend-per-charge-session: they eat, use the restroom, shop, and
relax while charging. A technical stop becomes a real break.

----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT THE WEBSITE DOES (three jobs in one)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. PITCH  — explain the vision credibly to outsiders.
2. PLAN   — lay out the offering, attractions, roadmap.
3. TRACK  — tick off build milestones (saved in the browser),
            plus a private, PIN-gated cost tracker for the owner.

----------------------------------------------------------------
TECH STACK (what we actually built)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS v4 (component-based, fast dev).
- Single-page site with smooth-scroll anchor nav.
- Persistence via localStorage (milestone checkboxes + budget),
  guarded in try/catch so it never breaks if storage is blocked.
- Responsive and accessible; deploys free to GitHub Pages / Netlify.
- All editable content lives in ONE file: src/data/plan.js.

----------------------------------------------------------------
OFFERINGS
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Restaurant: specialty coffee + standard Thakali set (the anchor),
  with an "Economical access" budget tier in a later phase.
- EV Charging: 1 × 60 kW dual-gun DC fast charger — two connectors,
  charges 2 vehicles at once (~30 min). (Full-build option scales to
  2 units later.)
- Attractions (see below) — the reasons to choose this stop.

----------------------------------------------------------------
ATTRACTIONS (build as budget allows; phase what's needed first)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Spotless 24/7 restrooms · free fast WiFi · live Thakali kitchen ·
EV waiting lounge · secure CCTV parking · convenience mart ·
pharmacy + first aid · ATM · kids play zone · photo/selfie point ·
quiet/prayer room · driver rest area + shower · tyre air pump ·
phone charging lockers · Tharu craft & souvenir nook ·
loyalty app / QR ordering · reserve-a-charger · garden seating.

----------------------------------------------------------------
ROADMAP (Phase 1)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Finalize location & lease → business registration & permits →
prepare loan proposal/financials → secure business loan →
electrical/grid connection → procure 60 kW dual-gun charger →
construction/fit-out → hire & train staff → soft launch →
grand opening (24/7).
Future phases: economical food tier · second site · loyalty +
reserve-a-charger app.

----------------------------------------------------------------
BUDGET (admin-only, /#admin + PIN)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Two switchable scenarios, both editable, with live auto-summing
totals saved in the browser:
  • LEAN (default, under NPR 1 crore): one 60 kW dual-gun charger,
    ~1,200 sq ft basic build, essential kitchen. Total ≈ NPR 93 lakh.
  • FULL build (~NPR 2.4 crore): ~2,500 sq ft, 2 charger units,
    full amenities — the eventual target once funded.
Figures are June-2026 market-research midpoints (NOT vendor quotes);
sources are listed in the tracker. Funded via business loan.
NOTE: the PIN gate only hides costs from casual viewers — it runs
in the browser and is not strong security.

----------------------------------------------------------------
DESIGN DIRECTION
----------------------------------------------------------------
Mood: a well-lit highway stop that's open when others are closed —
trustworthy for lenders, warm for hospitality, modern for EV/tech.
Green "leaf" base palette, with subtle amber (the night glow) and
mint accents on the EV/energy elements.
